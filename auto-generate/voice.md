# Voice profile — @aiforall.daily

- **Handle:** @aiforall.daily

- **Audience:** Students, working professionals, creators and small-business owners.
- **What it sells:** No product or service has been specified. Do not invent an offer.

## What the content should sound like
- Clear, conversational, curious and useful.
- Explain technical terms in everyday language.
- Prefer a quick demonstration and a concrete use case over hype.
- Keep spoken sentences short and easy to say aloud.
- English and natural Hindi (Devanagari) are both supported.
- Keep product names in their official English spelling.
- Avoid fake urgency, exaggerated claims and generic "AI will change everything" language.
- Do not invent personal stories, results, follower counts, savings or testimonials.
- Do not claim that a simulated demo is a real product screenshot or verified output.

## On-camera style
- Default format: faceless explainers with voiceover and screen demonstrations.
- Keep hooks short, direct and specific.
- Use one clear idea per Reel.
- One CTA per post, normally follow, save or comment a relevant keyword.

## Proof and boundaries
- No personal performance numbers have been supplied.
- Use only sourced, verifiable product facts and user-provided results.
- If a claim cannot be verified, qualify it or omit it.

## Caption preferences
